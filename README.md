🍕 Pizza Sales Dashboard (Excel)

The Pizza Sales Dashboard is an interactive Excel project designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, and product trends.
It helps in making data-driven decisions by providing clear insights into revenue, orders, and sales patterns.

## 📌 Key Insights

### 💰 Sales Performance

- **Total Revenue:** ₹8,17,860  
- **Total Orders:** 48,620  
- **Total Pizza Sold:** 49,574  
- **Average Order Value:** ₹16.82  

---

### 📅 Busiest Days & Time

- **Highest Orders:** Friday & Saturday  
- **Peak Time:** 12 PM – 1 PM  
- **Evening Rush:** 5 PM – 8 PM  

---

### 📈 Trends Analysis

- **Highest Order Day:** Friday  
- **Peak Sales Time:** Evening hours  
- **Lowest Activity:** Late night  

---

### 🍕 Sales by Category

- **Top Category:** Classic  
- **Second:** Supreme  
- **Third:** Veggie  
- **Fourth:** Chicken  

---

### 📏 Sales by Size

- **Highest Sales:** Large  
- **Second:** Medium  
- **Third:** Regular  
- **Lowest:** X-Large  

---

### 🏆 Best Sellers

- **Top 1:** Classic Deluxe Pizza  
- **Top 2:** Barbecue Chicken Pizza  
- **Top 3:** Pepperoni Pizza  

---

### ⚠️ Worst Sellers

- **Lowest 1:** Brie Carre Pizza  
- **Lowest 2:** Calabrese Pizza  
- **Lowest 3:** Spinach Supreme Pizza
